Fold Zine

Turn up to eight pictures into a one-sheet, 8-page folded zine. Everything runs on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Pages (1 = front cover, 8 = back cover)

Click a page to choose a picture, or drop images on it. Drag the ⋮⋮ handle to reorder pages.

Print sheet preview

How to fold it
  1. Print the sheet on one piece of paper (set the printer to actual size / 100%, landscape).
  2. Fold it in half the long way, then in half again the other way, and once more so you have eight panels. Unfold flat.
  3. Fold it in half the long way again, printed side out. Cut along the red line: the centre crease across the middle two panels only.
  4. Open it up, then fold the other way so the cut forms a gap. Push the two ends together until it folds into a cross shape.
  5. Fold the cross into a booklet with the front cover on top, and crease it flat.

Fold Zine is a self-hosted tool. Your pictures are processed in your browser and never leave your device. Works offline after the first load.
